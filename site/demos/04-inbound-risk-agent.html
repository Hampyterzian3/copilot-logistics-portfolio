Inbound Risk Early-Warning Agent

Late or lost inbound shipments are often noticed only when the customer calls. Contoso Logistics scores every in-transit shipment every 30 minutes on seven factors, explains each score in plain words and alerts the control tower in Teams before the promised date is missed. Click a shipment to see why it scores as it does. All data is synthetic.

Inbound risk monitor | Contoso Logistics
How this maps to Microsoft in production.
  • Shipment list and scores: a Power Automate scheduled flow runs every 30 minutes, pulls new tracking events from the TMS or visibility platform through a custom connector, and calls the scoring code (an Azure Function running risk_model.py, or a Dataverse plug-in). Scores, bands and reasons are written to Dataverse. On this page the JavaScript port scores the same synthetic features and is checked against the Python output.
  • KPI tiles and backtest: a Power BI risk dashboard on the Dataverse tables, with the monthly backtest (precision and recall of each band against actual delivery outcomes) as a report page.
  • Alert card: the same flow posts an Adaptive Card 1.5 to the control tower channel in Teams for every new critical shipment. Acknowledge, Draft customer update and Escalate to carrier start child flows and update the Dataverse alert row.
