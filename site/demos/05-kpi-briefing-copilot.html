Executive KPI Briefing Copilot

Leadership gets dashboards but not the story, and analysts spend Monday mornings writing the weekly account brief by hand. This demo shows a governed weekly KPI view for Contoso Logistics' six top customers (fictional, synthetic data), a variance engine that ranks what is material, and a drafted brief, slide outline and Teams post in which every sentence links back to the number it came from. Briefing agent
Simulation of the Copilot Studio agent: deterministic keyword intents over the exported variances. No live model.
How this maps to Microsoft 365 in production. KPI tiles, sparklines and the variance table: Power BI semantic model (single governed source, DAX measures for WoW, baseline and z-score). Monday export of flagged variances: Power Automate scheduled flow running a DAX query against the dataset and writing to Dataverse. Brief, slide outline and Teams post: Copilot Studio agent with Microsoft 365 Copilot drafting in Word and PowerPoint, from the variance rows only. Approval: Power Automate approvals in Teams; nothing reaches leadership until an analyst approves. Source tags: the fact ids map to named DAX measures, so each sentence can be checked in Power BI.
