Road-toll surcharge recovery

A new per-kilometre toll for heavy goods vehicles lands on every customer contract at once. This Power Apps style screen shows the toll impact per customer, lets a commercial manager pick a recovery method, shows the margin effect and routes the proposal to the right approver. Contoso Logistics, 2,000 synthetic shipments, 40 customers, 18 lanes.

Toll rates are ILLUSTRATIVE, invented for this demo by CO2 emission class and weight class. They are not an official tariff.

How this maps to Microsoft in production. Customer list, method picker and margin view: Power Apps canvas app with Power Fx over Dataverse tables Customers, Shipments, TollRates and SurchargeProposals. Toll and surcharge maths: Power Fx formulas (and the Python model for bulk recalculation of all contracts). Submit for approval and the card: Power Automate approval flow with tiered approvers, posted as an adaptive card in Teams. Dashboard tab: Power BI steering dashboard with DAX measures, embedded in the app. Code and documentation drafted with GitHub Copilot and reviewed by hand.
